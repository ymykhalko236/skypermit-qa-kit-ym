4. Unknowns

  1. U-01 What status code and message are returned when the duration is out of range? The text gives failing_gate TIME_WINDOW and rule_reference `TSD-4 §3` (line 190), and says failing_gate appears in a 422 response (line 30), but it never states the status code for this condition directly, and it gives no message text.
    Affects: EP-01, EP-03, BV-01, BV-02
  2. U-02 What is returned when window_end is not strictly after window_start (equal or earlier)? Is it the same duration failure (TIME_WINDOW, `TSD-4 §3`), or is it rejected earlier as an invalid request (for example when the draft is saved, with a different status)? The text gives no response for this condition.
    Affects: EP-04, EP-05, BV-03
  3. U-03 At what precision is the duration compared? The text uses whole minutes. How are windows with seconds handled (for example 14 min 59 s or 240 min 1 s), and are sub-minute timestamps accepted at all?
    Affects: EP-01, BV-01, BV-02, BV-03
  4. U-04 How are inputs the text does not define handled: a missing window_start or window_end, malformed timestamps, or timestamps that aren't in UTC?
    Affects: EP-01, EP-02, EP-03, EP-04, EP-05
  5. U-05 In what order are the TIME_WINDOW conditions (lead time, advance booking, duration, curfew, overlap) checked? If a window breaks the duration rule and another TIME_WINDOW condition at once, which rule_reference is reported? Until this is answered, every test must keep the other conditions (and the PILOT, AIRCRAFT and ZONE gates) satisfied.
    Affects: EP-01, EP-03, EP-04, EP-05, BV-01, BV-02, BV-03
